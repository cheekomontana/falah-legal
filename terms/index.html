1. What Falah is

Falah is an iPhone app that helps Muslims pray Salah on time. It calculates prayer times for your location, notifies you when a prayer arrives and when its window is closing, records the prayers you confirm, and — if you choose to use it — restricts distracting apps on your own device during a prayer window until you confirm that you have prayed.

Falah is a personal discipline tool. It is not a religious authority, and it does not replace one.

2. Prayer times are calculated estimates

This section matters more than any other, so please read it.

Falah calculates prayer times on your device using standard astronomical formulas and the calculation convention you select. It does not retrieve times from your local masjid or from any official body.

Calculated times are estimates. Reasonable, scholarly conventions differ from one another, sometimes by several minutes, and two people in the same room may correctly hold different times for Isha. Accuracy also depends on your device's location, clock and timezone being correct, and on the convention and madhab you have chosen. At very high latitudes, where the sun may not reach the required angle at all, Falah applies standard approximation rules that are themselves a matter of scholarly judgment.

You are responsible for your own worship. Where a time matters — and especially where Falah disagrees with your local masjid — follow your masjid or a qualified scholar, not this app. We make no warranty that any time shown is correct, and we accept no responsibility for a prayer prayed early, late, or missed while relying on the app.

3. Prayer Lock

Prayer Lock uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) system to restrict apps you select, on your own device, for a period beginning at a prayer time or at an hour you schedule. It ends when you confirm that you have prayed, or when the window elapses.

You should understand the following before relying on it:

  • It is a commitment device, not a security product. It exists to add friction to a habit you have told us you want to break. It is not designed to be unbreakable, and it can be ended at any time by turning it off in the app, revoking Screen Time permission in iOS Settings, or deleting Falah.
  • Do not rely on it where a locked app could matter urgently. While an app is restricted you may not see its notifications or content. Do not lock apps you may need in an emergency, for work, for medical purposes, for care responsibilities, or for anything time-critical. Choosing which apps to lock is your decision and your responsibility.
  • Phone calls, Messages and emergency services are never restricted by Falah. Falah restricts only the apps you select through Apple's picker.
  • It depends on Apple. Prayer Lock is built on iOS functionality that Apple controls and may change or withdraw. Restrictions are applied by iOS, not by us, and we cannot guarantee that they will always apply exactly when expected.
  • The confirmation is an oath you give yourself. Falah cannot verify that you prayed and does not try to. Confirming that you prayed when you have not defeats the only mechanism the app has.

4. The Companion

The Companion is the chat tab dedicated to establishing Salah. It answers only on that subject.

Its answers are generated by an artificial intelligence model — currently Google's Gemini — which we instruct to stay within Salah and to draw only on a fixed, checked list of Qur'anic verses and hadith. Using it requires an internet connection, a signed-in account, and is limited to 20 messages per hour.

You should understand what that means:

  • AI output can be wrong. A language model can state something inaccurate, misattribute a narration, or word a religious point poorly, and it can do so fluently and with apparent confidence. We constrain it as tightly as we can; we cannot guarantee any particular answer is correct.
  • Verify anything you rely on or pass on. This applies especially to a verse or hadith reference. Do not repeat something the Companion told you as established religious knowledge without checking it.
  • It is general encouragement, not a fatwa or a religious ruling, and not advice tailored to your circumstances. It is not a substitute for a qualified scholar, an imam, or your local community.
  • It is not professional advice. Not medical, mental health, legal, financial, or emergency advice. If you are struggling with something serious, please speak to a qualified person.
  • What you type is sent to a third party in order to be answered. Do not enter personal, sensitive, or confidential information about yourself or anyone else. See the Privacy Policy for exactly where it goes and what is kept.

You agree not to use the Companion to attempt to make it produce content outside its purpose, to abuse or overload the service, or to extract its underlying instructions.
